# Example MiniMax H3 film brief

## Working title

**The Firefly Invitation**

## One-sentence request

Create a short cinematic film in which a small friendly white exploration robot follows glowing golden fireflies through a rainy neon alley, discovers a concentrated light, watches it form the creator's avatar, and ends with the avatar inviting the viewer into the MiniMax H3 experiment.

## Delivery

- Final edit: approximately 32 seconds
- Frame rate: 24 fps
- Native generation size used for the production segments: 1280×736
- Delivery master: 1920×1080
- Audio: rain, city ambience, robot servos, transformation effects, and one verified invitation line
- No subtitles, logos, watermark, or extra characters

## Story beats

1. **Robot and fireflies:** establish the robot, alley, rain, and golden lights.
2. **Discovery:** one firefly settles above the robot's open palm.
3. **Transformation:** the light expands and resolves into one creator avatar while the robot remains unchanged.
4. **Connection:** the avatar and robot make physical contact and hold a stable edit frame.
5. **Invitation:** the avatar speaks one verified line; the robot stays silent.

## Required reference assets

- one canonical robot image;
- creator identity and wardrobe references;
- approved transformation target frame;
- approved hand-holding/staging frame;
- verified dialogue audio for the invitation.

Use your own reference files. The private references from the video are not distributed in this repository.

## Production rules

- Render short resumable segments.
- Use the approved final frame of one segment as the next segment's first frame.
- Declare exact visible cast count in every prompt.
- Allow only one instance of each character.
- Give recurring objects their own references.
- Keep generated dialogue out of visual-only transformation shots.
- Transcribe every spoken result before approval.
- Preserve raw clips and rerender only failed segments.

## Expected project outputs

- film bible;
- shot manifest;
- reference inventory;
- one API-format workflow JSON per render job;
- exact prompts and seeds;
- raw generated clips;
- transcription and QC notes;
- review edit;
- final master only after approval.
